# Notices

Contact Desk is a Lookbk skill. The section's design, its stylesheet, the
browser runtime (time zones, reply times, the rota, the plans, the form and
its states), the build, checks, server and recorder, the two examples and
this documentation were written for Lookbk.

Brask Moura Architects and Orrin Labs are invented. Their names, people,
hours, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, plans and copy are made up
for the demonstration; the email addresses and endpoints use the reserved
.example domain.

The doorstep plans, the clock, the rota and every mark are drawn in inline
SVG and CSS by the kit. No images, video or icons are shipped.

Included here:

- `scripts/kit/cdp.mjs`, `scripts/kit/serve.mjs` and `scripts/chrome.mjs`
  are Lookbk's shared helpers from the Motion Heroes, Scroll Cinema and
  Closing CTA Kit packages.

No third-party code is vendored or loaded: the runtime is Lookbk's own and
has no dependencies.

Fetched when a built page is opened, not shipped here, each from Google
Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and copyright its authors:

- **Spectral**, **Albert Sans** and **Fragment Mono** (the `spectral` voice)
- **Geist** and **Geist Mono** (the `geist` voice)
- **Hanken Grotesk** and **JetBrains Mono** (the `hanken` voice)

Used on your machine, not shipped: Chrome, Chromium or Edge (driven over the
DevTools protocol) and ffmpeg.
