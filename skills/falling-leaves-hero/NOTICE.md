# Notices

Falling Leaves Hero is a Lookbk skill. The hero design, the Motion Heroes
kit v2 (build, checks, runtime, recorder), the leaf atlas painted in code
(maple and ginkgo outlines, veins, mottling, focus levels), the backdrops,
the sign, the leaf shader (transmission, sheen, depth of field), the
falling paths, the landing solver and its slide, the gust, the coverage and
still scripts, the baked stills, the examples and this documentation were
written for Lookbk.

The falling paths follow published descriptions of how plates and leaves
fall (flutter, tumble and spiral regimes; for example Field, Klaus, Moore
and Nori, Nature 1997; Andersen, Pesavento and Wang, Journal of Fluid
Mechanics 2005). No code or data from them is used.

Fetched when a built page is opened, not shipped here (pinned versions,
checked with subresource-integrity hashes): three.js r180 from
cdn.jsdelivr.net (MIT licence, copyright the three.js authors), and the
fonts from cdn.jsdelivr.net (Fontsource packages), each under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 and copyright its authors. The examples use Fraunces, Big
Shoulders Display, IBM Plex Mono and Inter.

Already on your machine, used by the scripts: Chrome, Chromium or Edge
(headless) and ffmpeg (a separate program, under its own licence).

The brands (Aldermoor, Lantern Row Books), their places and copy in
`examples/` are made up.
