# Notices

Closing CTA Kit is a Lookbk skill. The three layouts (stub, poster and
sentence), the stylesheet, the browser runtime, the build, checks, server
and recorder, the two examples (Morrow Clay Studio and Fernhall Audio, which
are invented: their names, copy, prices, dates, counts, reviews, people and
addresses are not real) and this documentation were written for Lookbk.

The rubber stamp, the perforation, the seat dots, the barcode block and the
price window are drawn in CSS and inline SVG by the kit. No images, video or
icons are shipped.

Included here:

- `scripts/kit/cdp.mjs`, `scripts/kit/serve.mjs` and `scripts/chrome.mjs`
  are Lookbk's shared helpers from the Footage Heroes, Scroll Cinema and
  Footer Kit packages.

No third-party code is vendored or loaded: the runtime is Lookbk's own.

Fetched when a built page is opened, not shipped here, each from Google
Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and copyright its authors:

- **Big Shoulders Display**, **Schibsted Grotesk** and **IBM Plex Mono**
  (the `shoulders` display)
- **Archivo** and **JetBrains Mono** (the `archivo` display)
- **Newsreader**, with Schibsted Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono (the
  `newsreader` display)

Used on your machine, not shipped: Chrome, Chromium or Edge (driven over the
DevTools protocol) and ffmpeg.
