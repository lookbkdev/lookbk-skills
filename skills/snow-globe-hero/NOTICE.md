# Notices

Snow Globe Hero is a Lookbk skill. The hero design, the Motion Heroes kit
v2 (build, checks, runtime, recorder), the set solved from the layout, the
room, the plinth and its V-cut inscription, the brass card holder, the
traced glass sphere (refraction, dispersion, total internal reflection,
the air cap and meniscus, the shadow and caustic), the miniatures (the
café and the observatory), the flake and glitter simulation and drawing,
the baked stills in `assets/`, the examples and this documentation were
written for Lookbk.

No library is used: the page draws with raw WebGL1 and five small
programs (the room and the object, drawn once per size; a height map of
the miniature, drawn once; the frame and the flakes, every frame); the
headline's distance field is computed on a 2D canvas and uploaded as a
texture.

Fetched when a built page is opened, not shipped here (pinned versions,
checked with subresource-integrity hashes): the fonts from
cdn.jsdelivr.net (Fontsource packages), each under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 and copyright its authors. The examples use Fraunces, Jost,
Inter and IBM Plex Mono.

Already on your machine, used by the scripts: Chrome, Chromium or Edge
(headless) and ffmpeg (a separate program, under its own licence).

The brands (Alder & Thaw, Pell Ridge), their places, products, numbers and
copy in `examples/` are made up.
