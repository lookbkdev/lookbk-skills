# Notices

Aperture Reveal Hero is a Lookbk skill. The hero design, the Motion Heroes
kit v2 (build, checks, runtime, recorder), the iris (nine blades on pins
solved in closed form, the opening as the intersection of their edge
discs, the shingled seams, the bevels), the two scenes (the pier at night
and the cloister courtyard at noon), the depth of field, the bokeh drawn in
the iris's own shape, the diffraction stars, the clicking aperture ring
and its loop, the letters and the guard behind them, the legibility check,
the baked still pictures, the examples and this documentation were written
for Lookbk.

No library is used: the page draws with raw WebGL2 and two fragment
shaders; the headline and the scale are drawn on a 2D canvas and uploaded
as a texture.

Fetched when a built page is opened, not shipped here (pinned versions,
checked with subresource-integrity hashes): the fonts from
cdn.jsdelivr.net (Fontsource packages), each under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 and copyright its authors. The examples use Jost, IBM Plex
Sans, Fraunces, Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono.

Already on your machine, used by the scripts: Chrome, Chromium or Edge
(headless) and ffmpeg (a separate program, under its own licence).

The brands (Thale Optical and its Nocta 50, Corte Lenta), their places,
numbers and copy in `examples/` are made up.
