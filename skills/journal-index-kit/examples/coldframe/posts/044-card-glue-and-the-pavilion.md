Over six weeks we made eleven models of a garden pavilion. The client looked at every one of them with care, asked good questions, and in the end decided to plant an orchard instead.

We think that was the right decision, and the models helped them make it. By the eighth model it was clear that the best place for the pavilion was where the old apple trees had been, and by the tenth the client said, gently, that they would rather have the trees back.

We keep all eleven on a shelf in the studio. They are a record of a design process that worked even though nothing was built, which is a sentence we did not expect to write when we started the job.
