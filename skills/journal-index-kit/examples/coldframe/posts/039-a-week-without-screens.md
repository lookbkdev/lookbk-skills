dek: We drew everything by hand for five days. Two habits stuck.

In the first week of December, when the work is quiet, we put the laptops in a drawer and drew everything by hand. Five days, four people, one rule: if it needs a screen, it waits until Monday.

Most of what we learned was predictable. Hand drawing is slower and makes you commit earlier. Two things surprised us enough to keep:

1. We now begin every project with a single drawing at 1:20, by hand, before anything is modelled. It forces the important decisions into the first afternoon.
2. We pin the week’s drawings on the wall on Friday and talk about them standing up. It takes twenty minutes and replaces a meeting that used to take an hour.

The laptops came back out on Monday. The wall stayed.
