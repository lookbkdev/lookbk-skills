Every edge on a piece of furniture makes a small promise about how it will feel. A sharp arris promises precision and delivers splinters. A heavy round-over promises comfort and delivers a kind of vagueness, as if the maker could not decide where the object ended.

For years we used a 3 mm radius on everything, because it was safe. It took the sharpness off without anyone noticing, and that was exactly what was wrong with it. An edge nobody notices is an edge nobody designed.

This year we are going to look at edges properly: on benches, on stairs, on thresholds, on the hems of curtains. One piece at a time, in the order we meet them in our own work. This is the first.
