The banquette in the reading room is covered in felt from a single flock, forty minutes from the studio. We know the farmer, and he knows the sheep, which is not a sentence that applies to most upholstery.

Felt is unforgiving to specify. It has no weave to hide behind, so every variation in the fleece shows. We asked for that. The undyed swatch on the top left of the board is the colour of the field in late summer, and it is the one we used for the seat.

The dyed swatches are for the cushions: madder for the rust, weld over-dyed with indigo for the green, and walnut hulls for the brown. They will fade, slowly and evenly, and we have told the client so. Fading is the felt remembering where it has been.
