Our old rota was a week long and started on Friday afternoons, which meant every shift began with a weekend. We changed three things.

1. Shifts are now three and a half days, Monday to Thursday morning and Thursday to Monday morning.
2. Every shift has a named secondary who is expected to answer within fifteen minutes, so the primary can sleep through the night if nothing is on fire.
3. Handover happens on a call, not in a document, and takes ten minutes.

Pages per shift have not changed. How people feel about the shift has, and we will write about that in the next part.
