# Notices

Phone Screen Showcase is a Lookbk skill. The section design, the phone
(drawn in HTML and CSS), the app-screen blocks (lists, tiles, charts, chips,
toggles, calendar, seat map, poster cards, ticket, buttons), the line icons,
the window and projector lights, the runtime, the build, check and record
scripts, both examples and this documentation were written for Lookbk. No
libraries are vendored and the built page has no JavaScript dependencies.

The phone is drawn generically: no maker's name, logo, camera layout or
other brand mark of any real device.

Fernhouse and Latecomer are invented brands. Every name, place, film,
director, price, date and figure in the examples is made up for the
demonstration. The posters and the ticket art are drawn in CSS; the examples
use no photographs.

Included here:

- `scripts/kit/cdp.mjs`, `scripts/kit/serve.mjs` and `scripts/chrome.mjs`
  are Lookbk's shared helpers from the Motion Heroes and Phone Trio Hero
  packages.

Fetched when a built page is opened, not shipped here:

- **Young Serif**, **Figtree** and **IBM Plex Mono** (Fernhouse), and
  **Big Shoulders Display**, **Geist** and **Geist Mono** (Latecomer), from
  Google Fonts, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and copyright its
  authors.

Used on your machine, not shipped: Chrome, Chromium or Edge (driven over the
DevTools protocol) and ffmpeg (for the video).
